2. Payment data — what we never see

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number, CVV and authentication details go directly to Stripe and are never sent to, processed by, or stored on our servers. We receive only a customer reference, your subscription status, and limited details such as the card's last four digits and expiry, to show you what you are paying with.

Stripe processes this data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

3. Your resume, and who it is sent to

Your resume is used in two ways, and they are different:

  • Job matching runs on our own server. Your resume is not sent anywhere outside Craxle for this.
  • AI features (apply kit, job-description match) send the relevant text of your resume, plus the job posting, to a third-party AI provider to generate suggestions. Providers currently used are Google (Gemini) and Groq. They process it to return a response.

Because of this, do not put data in your resume that you would not want sent to an AI provider — for example your Aadhaar number, passport number, bank details or government ID. Your name, email, phone, education and work history are ordinarily expected on a resume and are fine.

We never send your resume to an employer. You apply yourself.

4. How we use your data

To run the service: sign you in, save your progress, match you to jobs, generate application material, take payment, provide support, prevent abuse, and meet legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show advertising. We do not share your resume or profile with recruiters or employers.

5. Legal basis (for users in the EU/UK)

We process your data to perform our contract with you (running your account, matching, and paid features); on legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, improving the service); on legal obligation (tax and accounting records); and on consent where you optionally provide extra profile details or upload a resume — consent you can withdraw at any time by deleting them.

8. How long we keep it

  • Billing and tax records — retained as long as Indian tax law requires, currently up to 8 years, even after you close your account.
