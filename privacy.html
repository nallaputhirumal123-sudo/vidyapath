2. Payment data — what we never see

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number, CVV and authentication details go directly to Stripe and are never sent to, processed by, or stored on our servers. We receive only a customer reference, your subscription status, and limited details such as the card's last four digits and expiry.

Stripe acts as an independent controller for that data under its own policy: stripe.com/privacy. Invoices and tax records are retained as law requires.

3. Your resume, and who it is sent to

Your resume is used two ways, and they are different:

  • Job matching runs on our own server. Your resume is not sent anywhere outside Craxle for this.
  • AI features (apply kit, job-description match) send the relevant text of your resume, plus the job posting, to a third-party AI provider — currently Google (Gemini) and Groq — to generate suggestions.

Because of this, do not put data in your resume that you would not want sent to an AI provider — for example Aadhaar or national ID numbers, passport numbers or bank details. Name, email, phone, education and work history are ordinarily expected on a resume and are fine.

We never send your resume to an employer. You apply yourself.

3a. Being found by employers (optional, off by default)

You can choose to let employers search for you. This is off unless you switch it on in your profile, and you can switch it off again at any time. If you never turn it on, nothing in this section applies to you and no employer can see you at all.

When it is on, an employer who pastes a job description sees a ranked, anonymous result: your matching skills, the skills you are missing, your seniority, your role areas, your engagement preference (for example contract or full-time), and a match score. They do not see your name, email address, phone number, employer history or your resume file. You are identified only by a random reference that cannot be linked back to your account by them.

An employer may request an introduction. You decide. Your contact details are shared only if you accept, and declining is not shown to them as a rejection. We do not sell your data, and an employer cannot download, export or buy a list of candidates.

Switching this off removes you from all future searches immediately. Employers you have already accepted an introduction from will still hold what you chose to send them, which we cannot recall on your behalf — ask them directly, and tell us if they do not comply.

4. The browser extension

The Craxle Autofill extension is optional. If you install it:

  • It pairs using a single-use code that expires in 10 minutes. No session cookie or login token is ever given to the extension, so it cannot act as you or sign in as you.
  • The details you use to fill forms — name, email, phone, address, links, education, work authorisation — are stored in your own browser (chrome.storage.local). They are not sent to us when you fill a form, and we do not receive a record of the forms you fill.
  • It reads the page you are on only at the moment you click "Fill this form". It has no background process and does not run on any site otherwise.
  • It never submits an application and never contacts an employer.
  • "Disconnect" erases those details from your browser immediately.

To run the service: sign you in, save progress, match you to jobs, generate application material, take payment, provide support, prevent abuse, and meet legal, accounting and tax obligations.

We do not sell or share your personal data for advertising or cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not show ads. We do not give your resume or profile to recruiters or employers.

9. How long we keep it

  • Account and learning data — while your account is active.
  • Uploaded resume text — until you remove it or delete your account. The "Remove" button on the jobs page clears it immediately.
  • Extension details — in your browser until you press Disconnect or uninstall. We never hold a copy.
  • Job listings — refreshed regularly; roughly 7 days of history.
  • Billing, invoice and tax records — retained as long as US federal and state tax law requires (currently up to 7 years), even after you close your account. We cannot delete these on request.

10. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to stop a particular use. Email support@craxle.com and we will respond within 30 days. We do not charge for this and will not treat you differently for asking.

If you are in India

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 gives you the right to access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed, to have it corrected, completed or updated, to have it erased, to nominate somebody to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated, and to a grievance process.

Raise a grievance with us first at the email above. We are your grievance contact and will answer within 30 days. If we do not resolve it, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If you are in the EU or UK

You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your local supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

11. Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in — a secure session token, not your password. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Stripe may set its own cookies during checkout for fraud prevention, and Cloudflare may set a security cookie.

12. Security

Passwords are hashed, connections encrypted in transit, and access to the production database restricted. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

13. Children

Most of the people who learn here are children, so this section is the one that matters most.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, anybody under 18 is a child. We process a child's personal data only with verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian. Where a school, college or coaching centre enrols students, the institution obtains and holds that consent and confirms to us that it has done so; we act on the institution's instructions for those accounts.

We do not track children and we do not advertise to them. The Act forbids behavioural monitoring of children and targeted advertising directed at them, and there is no advertising, no ad network, no behavioural profiling and no third-party analytics anywhere in this product for anybody, of any age.

An account created from a class code holds as little as possible. A student signs in by tapping their name on their class register. That account carries the name their school typed and nothing else: no email address that reaches them, no password they could reuse elsewhere, and no phone number. The address on it is synthetic and cannot receive mail.

The job half is closed to them. Accounts created from a class code, and any account whose stated date of birth is under 18, cannot reach job listings, employer features or resume sharing at all. That is enforced on the server, not merely hidden in the app.

Paid subscriptions are for adults aged 18 and over.

A parent, guardian or the institution may ask us to erase a child's account and everything attached to it by writing to the address below. We do this without argument and without charge.
