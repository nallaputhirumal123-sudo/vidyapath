## 4. What I need answered

### A. Children's data under the DPDP Act 2023 — my main concern

1. Section 9 requires **verifiable parental consent** before processing a
   child's personal data. My assumption is that a **school can obtain and hold
   that consent** on the parents' behalf and confirm it to me, and that I may
   then act on the school's instructions. **Is that assumption correct?** If it
   is, what exactly must the school-facing contract say for me to be able to
   rely on it, and what evidence of consent must I require or retain?
2. Is my class-code sign-in (name only, no email, no password) **compatible**
   with the Act, and does it help or hurt on data minimisation?
3. The Act prohibits tracking, behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising
   directed at children. I do none of these for anyone. **Is there anything
   further I must document or declare** to rely on that?
4. Are there **exemptions** available to me (for example, any notified
   exemption for educational institutions or for processing that is verifiably
   safe) that would change my obligations?
5. What are the **penalties** if I have got any of this wrong? I understand
   they are substantial and I would like to know the actual exposure.

### B. My relationship with the school

6. Am I a **Data Fiduciary** in my own right, or a **Data Processor** acting on

7. Please tell me what a **school agreement / data processing agreement** must

8. If a parent asks the school to delete their child's account and everything
