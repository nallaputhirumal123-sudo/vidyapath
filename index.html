Jobs, matching, reading the posting and Ask Axle are free; $9.99 a month covers the apply kit, per-job interview prep, the Axle Pro board, the tracker, alerts and the extension.

No — we prepare the application and you submit it yourself on the employer's own site.

It is arithmetic, not a guess — it names the skills you have, the ones you are missing, and the years and visa lines it found.
